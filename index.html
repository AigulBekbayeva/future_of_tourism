About this geoportal

Three linked maps for the challenge The Future of Tourism. All indicators are calculated from open data; the processing scripts are in the scripts/ folder of the repository.

Climate comfort (maps 01–02)

  • Weather today. Daily mean temperature, relative humidity and precipitation for 2020–2025 from the ERA5 reanalysis, averaged over 3,610 first-level administrative regions (GADM 3.6) by the AREAdata project, plus 17 small territories without sub-national units.
  • Bias adjustment. AREAdata temperature comes from the 1000 hPa level, which runs warm over high ground. Each region's series is shifted so its mean matches the WorldClim 2.1 near-surface climate for 2021–2040 (SSP2-4.5, median of the models); day-to-day variability is kept.
  • Comfortable day. Precipitation ≤ 5 mm, no heat stress, and a daily mean temperature of 15–24 °C (sightseeing & city) or 23–30 °C (beach & sun). The bands follow the ideal ranges of the Holiday Climate Index (Scott et al., 2016), translated from daytime maximum to daily mean.
  • Heat-stress day. Daily mean humidex ≥ 42 — the afternoon peak is then well inside the “great discomfort / dangerous” range.
  • Future. Warming of each region between 2021–2040 and 2041–2060 or 2081–2100 from WorldClim 2.1 downscaled CMIP6 (up to 9 models) is added to the daily temperatures of 2020–2025 (delta method). Values shown are the median across models; the range across models is shown in the region card. Humidity and precipitation are held at present-day values.

Tourism economy (map 03)

  • Tourism receipts, % of exports — World Bank WDI ST.INT.RCPT.XP.ZS, latest pre-pandemic year (2015–2019).
  • Tourism receipts, % of GDP — the share above multiplied by exports of goods and services as % of GDP (NE.EXP.GNFS.ZS) in the same year. It measures earnings from foreign visitors only; domestic tourism is not included.
  • Population — SP.POP.TOTL, latest year. Climate change — change in comfortable days (either profile) averaged over a country's regions, weighted by population.

Limitations

Regional averages smooth out coasts and mountains inside large regions; sunshine, wind and sea temperature are not included; humidity and rainfall are not projected; six years of weather is a short baseline. Near a threshold, small warming can shift many days at once. The maps describe climate suitability, not actual tourist demand.
